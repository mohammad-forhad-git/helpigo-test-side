Helpigo: On-Demand Errand & Task Hiring Platform

Helpigo

Your Task, Our People.

Hire a verified local helper for medicine, groceries, fresh-market shopping, pick-ups and any everyday errand — booked by you, or for your family.

Dhaka pilotBangla + EnglishInstallable web app
Helper: tasks near you
Customer: live task + code
Helper: list & earnings
Problem → Solution

Problem: Busy professionals, elderly people, patients and families living away cannot easily get small errands done. Delivery apps sell only their own catalogue, and informal help (a neighbour, a building guard) has no receipt, no record and no accountability.

Solution: One platform where anyone posts any permitted errand, sees the price first, and an ID-verified helper nearby does it — with in-app approval for price changes, receipt proof, a 4-digit delivery code, and cash or bKash/Nagad payment.

Key Functions

Any errand, price first

6 services + custom tasks; item list or photo of list/prescription; fee shown before booking.

Verified helpers

NID + selfie + reference checked by staff; ratings both ways; SOS on every task.

Smart matching

By zone, service, helper purchase limit and female-helper preference; race-safe accept.

Proof & delivery code

Receipt photo, approvals for any extra spend, task closes only with the customer's code.

Cash, bKash, Nagad

Helper ledger, dues cap, weekly payouts, prepay for big baskets, refunds as credit.

Ops back-office

Helper review, live tasks, disputes, payments, reports vs pilot targets, chat & alerts.

Process Flow

1. Post task

Items, shop, address, time, budget — price shown

2. Matched

Nearby verified helper accepts or makes an offer

3. Shop & prove

Receipt photo; approval needed for any change

4. Hand over

At the door, completed with the 4-digit code

5. Pay & rate

Cash/wallet; earnings & fees settled in ledgers
